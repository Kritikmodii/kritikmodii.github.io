About

I'm a software engineer who likes the unglamorous parts of the job: the migration that has to run at 3am without waking anyone up, the API that's boring enough to trust, the internal tool that saves twenty minutes a day for a team of ten.

Most of my work lives on the backend — services, data pipelines, and the plumbing that holds a product together — but I'm equally comfortable pairing on frontend work or writing the CLI nobody asked for but everybody uses by week two.

Outside of client work I spend time on open source, write up what I learn, and keep a running list of ideas that are probably weekend projects and definitely not startups.
